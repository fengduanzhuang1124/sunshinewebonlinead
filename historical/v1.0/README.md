# Sunshine Health AI Skill Library V1.0

**Status:** Historical Version

## Development Stage

Initial unified AI advertising skill library.

V1.0 represents the earliest retained structured version of the Sunshine Health AI advertising workflow.

At this stage, advertisement generation, copywriting, product knowledge, product comparison, promotion planning, WeChat layout and brand visual consistency were maintained inside one broad skill library.

## Main Modules

1. Advertisement Generation
2. WeChat Moments Advertisement Copywriting
3. Product Knowledge Explanation
4. Product Comparison
5. Promotion Planning
6. WeChat Layout
7. Brand Visual Consistency

## Core Design Direction

The initial system established several important principles:

- Use real Sunshine Health product photographs.
- Preserve real product packaging.
- Preserve the real retail shelf environment.
- Do not invent products or accessories.
- Only display promotion information explicitly provided by the user.
- Maintain consistent Sunshine Health visual identity.

## Architecture Stage

Business Requirements  
→ Unified AI Skill Library

At this stage, recognition rules, advertisement rules, copywriting rules and brand rules were still maintained together rather than separated into specialised modules.

## Historical Status

This version was developed before formal Git version control was introduced.

The source version is preserved as historical evidence of the project's early development.

Its original development identity must not be changed or represented as a historical Git commit.

## Evolution

V1.0 later evolved into the V2.1 Production Design Specification.
